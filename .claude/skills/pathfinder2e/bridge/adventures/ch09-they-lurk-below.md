### cluster: return-candlemere — "Returning to Candlemere & the Spiral Seal"   (source: p.399 / L17829)   mode: sandbox
scene: Eons ago the misanthropic cultist Foras built a complex for his Yog-Sothoth cult, the Thresholders, beneath Candlemere; Pharasmins led by Foras's younger brother Korog took the tower but were thwarted in the depths, sealing the cult below with the Spiral Seal — then blasphemously becoming graveknights to guard it forever (p.399–400, L17831–17843). The PCs (16th level) return to a now-optional but rewarding dungeon: clearing it grants major protection against the warped time of Nyrissa's House at the Edge of Time in Ch.10 (p.399, L17849). Hooks to draw them back: recovering Irovetti's notes (+4 item bonus to research), or rumors of swirling will-o'-wisp lights / strange manifestations (p.399, L17859). They research the Spiral Seal via Gamemastery Guide Research rules (Library 16, DC 33 Library/Pharasma Lore, DC 37 Occultism, DC 35 Religion) to learn it can be unmade by Occultism/Religion, dispel magic, or physical damage — all risking backlash (p.400, L17863–17875). Korog's Heresies: DC 30 Religion / DC 25 Pharasma Lore reveals the graveknights' undeath is blasphemous, granting +2 to Demoralize and emotion-effect DCs against them (p.401, L17879). The Spiral Seal of Pharasma (area F6, Ch.2) is presented as Hazard 16: Disable DC 37 Occultism / DC 40 Religion (10-min ritual) or dispel magic (9th, DC 40); AC 39, Hardness 27, HP 106, recovery; a Backlash reaction (DC 41 Fort, prone + stupefied + doomed on failures) hits all within 60 ft when an attempt fails (p.400, L17889–17909). Breaking it vanishes a 10-ft stone disc, opening a 100-ft shaft (DC 30 Climb) down to A1; reward 60 XP (p.400, L17887). While sealed, a powerful aura blocks all teleport/tunneling in and out (one dream exception in Part 2); removing it lifts this but the occupants, lulled by millennia, likely won't notice for months/years (p.401, L17913–17915). Dungeon Features: brick-fit glowing walls, 10-ft halls / 20-ft chambers, heavy unlocked stone doors; the Ch.2 Dread Aura (p.144) persists below if undisabled (p.401, L17917–17919).

### cluster: spiral-investigation — "The Helix Theorem & the Spiral of Yog-Sothoth"   (source: p.413 / L18453)   mode: sandbox
scene: Candlemere's lowest level is the Spiral of Yog-Sothoth, rooms built around an enormous engineered spiral that focuses the Outer God's gaze; Foras works at its center to draw Yog-Sothoth into the Material Plane (p.413–414, L18457–18461). Months before the Pharasmin clash, Foras summoned envoys from the plateau of Leng, who got trapped by the Seal and now hold the eastern rooms (B5–B6) alongside their hounds of Tindalos (p.413, L18463–18469). The Spiral itself is the lasting danger: simply killing the cult won't stop it, because its shape is burned into reality (DC 35 Occultism / DC 33 Yog-Sothoth Lore to realize this — p.414, L18473). To neutralize it, the PCs must perform the Helix Theorem: close a prepared gate icon nine times, in order, in B14→B13→B12→B10→B8→B4→B3→B2→B1, each requiring a DC 36 Occultism check (others may Aid); any failure or out-of-sequence closing resets to B14; the closed gate ends in B1's alcove (p.413, L18475–18477). The icon is the miniature gate from B14 (or DC 38 Crafting to make one). Success twists the spiral rooms into a straight line, ending the threat and — unexpectedly — bolstering every present PC's place in time for the House at the Edge of Time (p.413, L18477–18481). Failure leaves the Spiral to draw new cultists or Yog-Sothoth's gaze, worsening the Ch.10 blooms (p.413, L18483). Reward 120 XP. The first clue is the A9 slate; full ritual steps come from the B13 mystics' brass tablets.
