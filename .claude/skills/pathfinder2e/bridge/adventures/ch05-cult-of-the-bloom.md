### cluster: cradle-climax — "Cradle of Lamashtu — Jin's Ghost & the Garden of the Bloom (C9–C10)"   (source: p.245–248 / L10839)   mode: keyed-rooms
scene: The walled-off climax. C9 holds the dry, mold-free cavern where the one-armed gnome ghost Jin Durwhimmer (Creature 9, unique, site-bound) lingers; non-cultists he begs for help, supplying the key lore — "defeat the bloom, and the scar will heal itself" — and, if promised aid, gifting one PC knowledge of three primal rituals once the bloom dies. C10, the Garden of the Bloom, is the misty, sickening (DC 25 Fort) source-chamber of the whole curse.
