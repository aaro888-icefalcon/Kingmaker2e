# Ingested Adventure — Kingmaker Chapter 10: Sound of a Thousand Screams   (hook: adventure-ingest)
fidelity: full   ·   source: Kingmaker AP 2e, Chapter 10, lines 18902–21427

The climactic chapter, in three parts. Part 1 "A Month of Destruction" (PCs ~17th level) is a SANDBOX of
the bloom-besieged kingdom: Nyrissa's planar breaches erupt one per few days across the Stolen Lands, each
a self-contained encounter linked to a glade in Thousandbreaths; defeating a bloom yields a "bloom trophy"
that aids the PCs in that linked glade. Part 2 "A Thousand Breaths" (PCs ~18th) sends the PCs through the
Castle of Knives portal into Nyrissa's demiplane — nine glades (B–J, plus the Byway A), each tethering
the realm; defeating glade guardians can "uproot" Thousandbreaths into the Material Plane. Part 3 "House at
the Edge of Time" (PCs ~19th) is KEYED-ROOMS through the planar manor (K/L/M levels + the four Fables M7a–d)
culminating in confronting Nyrissa — kill, forgive, or be banished. The sword *Briar* (forged from Nyrissa's
own fractured spirit) grows in power throughout and is her foretold doom. Diminisher and seed-lists at end.

### cluster: bloom-rules — "How the blooms work (the month engine)"   (source: p.425 / L19004)   mode: sandbox
scene: Part 1 plays out over a single 30-day month, blooms erupting every few days — fast and relentless, so the PCs can't recover between them; no kingdom management occurs once they begin. Each bloom manifests in one hex (never the capital), which is lost from the kingdom; it spreads 1 hex/day into a random adjacent hex, all reverting (but needing reclaiming) when the core bloom is defeated. A DC 36 Nature / DC 34 First World Lore check confirms a bloom is a First World planar breach. Most blooms cause rampant overgrowth (difficult terrain, +5 to Survival DCs). News arrives via the secret NOTICE BLOOM reaction (DC 35 Exploration / DC 30 Defense kingdom checks; auto-crit if in the capital or a PC's hex); the BLOOM WATCH downtime activity hunts undiscovered blooms. Defeating a bloom releases its energy in a vortex of green light and a keening cry, infusing nearby objects into BLOOM TROPHIES — items magically linked to a Thousandbreaths glade granting +1 status to most rolls there. The PCs need at least three different trophy types to open the Castle of Knives portal in Part 2.
