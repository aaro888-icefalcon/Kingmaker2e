# PF2e Character Creation — solo Kingmaker quick-guide

Run at Session Zero (the engine routes here from `adapt-character-creation.md` → writes
`campaigns/kingmaker/character-sheet.md`). Solo play uses **one PC**; build it a little tougher
than a party member (the engine and the **Diminisher** scale foes for one hero — see
`adventures/kingmaker-overview.md`). Optionally run a small **party of PCs** or add a
**companion/animal/familiar** for survivability.

## Build steps (Level 1)
1. **Concept** — who answers the swordlords' call, and why the Stolen Lands? Tie to a Kingmaker
   background below so the campaign is personal.
2. **Ancestry** (+ heritage) — ability boosts/flaw, HP, size, speed, ancestry feat. Common Stolen
   Lands ancestries: human, half-elf, half-orc, dwarf, elf, gnome (fey-touched), halfling. Rarer
   ancestries draw curious looks in Brevoy.
3. **Background** — two ability boosts + a skill (trained) + a Lore + a skill feat. Prefer a
   **Kingmaker background** (below) for setting hooks.
4. **Class** — key ability, class HP/level, initial proficiencies, class feat, class DC. All core
   classes fit; see the AP's per-class notes (uncommon classes — gunslinger/inventor — need GM ok).
5. **Ability scores** — start 10s; apply four boosts each from **ancestry, background, class key
   ability**, then **four free boosts** (no two to the same score in one set). A boost is +2 (or +1
   above 18). Aim for an 18 in your key ability.
6. **Skills** — trained in: your class's listed skills + background skill + **Int modifier** extra
   skills. Wilderness skills (**Nature, Survival**) and social skills (**Diplomacy, Deception,
   Intimidation**) shine in Kingmaker; **Lore** picks like Warfare, Politics, Brevoy, Pitax,
   First World, Hunting, Forest, Mercantile pay off later.
7. **Feats & details** — 1 ancestry feat, 1 class feat, 1 skill feat (background), general feat at
   3rd; HP = ancestry + (class + Con) ; AC, saves, Perception per proficiency; pick spells if a
   caster; buy gear from **15 gp** starting funds.
8. **Record** to `character-sheet.md` (copy the engine template `assets/templates/character-sheet.md`
   and fill PF2e fields: ancestry/heritage, background, class, level, abilities, AC, HP, saves,
   Perception, skills + proficiencies, attacks, spells, feats, items, and the PC's goals/bonds).

## Kingmaker backgrounds (pick one — each grants a setting hook)
These tie the PC to the Stolen Lands and seed early Threads/Characters:
- **Aspiring Free-Lord/Lady** — you came to *rule*; trained in a Lore of statecraft. (Kingdom hook.)
- **Bandit** — you fled the law or other bandits into the wilds; know ambush and survival.
- **Brevic Noble** — minor House blood; Politics Lore; a stake in Brevoy's tangled court.
- **Issian / Rostlandic patriot** — loyalties in Brevoy's two halves; a personal cause.
- **Pioneer / Sword Scion** — Aldori dueling tradition (Warfare Lore; access to Aldori dueling
  swords) or a settler's grit.
- **Hopeful Charter-Holder** — you hold (or covet) a charter of your own.
- **Local Tribesperson / Nomen-friend** — ties to the centaurs or wilderness peoples.
- (Any standard PF2e background also works — anchor it to a reason for heading south.)

## Solo-play notes
- The PC's **goals and bonds** become starting **Threads** (e.g. *Claim a barony*, *Avenge a
  family slight on Pitax*) — seed them into `threads.json` at Session Zero.
- Consider a hireling, animal companion, or a recruited NPC (e.g. **Akiros**) early — the Stolen
  Lands are lethal to a lone hero, and the kingdom needs leaders for its roles.
- Death/maiming/capture are real (engine discipline). Play cautious, scout, and use the terrain.
