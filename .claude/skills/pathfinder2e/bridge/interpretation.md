# Interpretation & GM Lens — The Stolen Lands (Kingmaker)   (hook: meaning)

Broad GM-craft for THIS world. NOT a reskin table. Read on every interpretation/NPC moment so
oracle and Meaning-Table results come out setting-true and NPCs act competently.

## Reading the oracle here
- **Two registers, one campaign.** Kingmaker is a **frontier-survival hexcrawl** that becomes a
  **kingdom-and-realm drama**. Early, read results gritty and grounded — weather, wilderness,
  bandits, hunger, exhaustion, the indifference of nature. Later, read them political and
  mythic — succession, war, fey caprice, the slow tightening of Nyrissa's curse.
- **Lean medium-literal.** The Stolen Lands are concrete: a "danger" is a real predator, a hostile
  hex, a rival's blade — not abstract dread. But hold one **uncanny thread** under everything:
  the First World presses on the Greenbelt, and small wrongnesses (a will-o'-wisp, a weeping
  statue, a fey bargain) are early echoes of the campaign's hidden spine.
- **The land remembers.** Ruins, cairns, and old kingdoms (the Forgotten Keep, Candlemere, the
  Nomen barrows, Vordakai's tomb) are everywhere. When a result wants history, the answer is
  usually *something fell here before you came, and it is not entirely gone.*
- **Player ≠ PC knowledge.** Nyrissa, the Lantern King, who funds the bandits — these are
  authored truths the PC has **not** earned. Treat them as only potential until discovered in
  play; never steer with them.

## NPCs & factions in this world (so they act to win)
- **Restov / the Aldori swordlords (Rostland, southern Brevoy)** — the patrons. Proud, martial,
  honor-bound duelists who chafe under House Surtova's northern crown. They want the Stolen Lands
  **claimed and loyal** — buffer, prestige, leverage. Lady Jamandi Aldori is generous to useful
  agents, ruthless with embarrassing ones. They reward results and despise being made to look weak.
- **Brevoy (House Surtova et al.)** — a fractured realm one bad winter from civil war; watches the
  new kingdom with calculation, not warmth. Useful as patron, dangerous as overlord.
- **Pitax & King Castruccio Irovetti** — the River-Kingdom rival to the south-west. A vain,
  cultured tyrant who fancies himself artist and conqueror; stifles real talent, buys propaganda,
  and works through **intermediaries and deniability** (he funds the early bandit pressure). He
  plays long, petty, and dirty; he will smile, gift, and stab. Never let him act stupidly —
  he is competent and insecure, the worst combination.
- **The bandits & the Stag Lord** — not random thugs but a brutal little proto-realm. The Stag
  Lord rules by fear, drink, and a cursed artifact; his lieutenants (Akiros, Dovan, Kressle) each
  have their own cracks to exploit. Bandits flee, regroup, take hostages, and inform — they act
  to survive.
- **The fey & the First World** — capricious, rule-bound, and *not human*. They keep bargains to
  the letter and punish the spirit; kindness and cruelty by their own logic. Tiressia, the
  fey pranksters, Nyrissa's agents — read them as alien wills, never as set dressing.
- **Tiger Lords (barbarian confederation)** and **Nomen centaurs** — proud wilderness peoples,
  potential allies or enemies depending on respect shown. They remember slights and debts.
- **Kingdom subjects** — once the PCs rule, the **people** become an NPC force: they want safety,
  food, justice, and meaning. Unrest is their voice. A ruler who ignores them earns Ruin.

## Good-GM advice for this campaign
- **The sandbox is the point.** Don't herd toward "the plot." Surface authored content by
  relevance (the cluster/fragment library), let threads pull, and let honest dice decide. The
  Stag Lord falling early, an "essential" NPC dying, a hex skipped — **adapt**; the map is not a
  script.
- **Stakes scale with the campaign's two registers** (survival → death/maiming/capture; realm →
  ruin, war, lost subjects, a kingdom's collapse). Honesty never relaxes; harshness tracks genre.
- **The kingdom is a character.** Tie Turning Points and Random Events to it — a far hex you never
  cleared becomes a monster attack on a village; a slighted faction becomes an Unrest event.
- **Foreshadow the curse sparingly.** Nyrissa should be felt as weather long before she is known
  as cause. Small fey wrongnesses, the recurring image of a many-petaled flower, a name half-heard.

## Term equivalences (only where helpful)
- Mythic "Magic Item" ≈ a PF2e item with a **rarity/level** (common/uncommon/rare/unique).
- Mythic "PC has the advantage" ≈ a circumstance bonus / off-guard target; "NPC advantage" ≈ a
  DC bump or the foe acting first.
- An Adventure-Crafter **Turning Point** ≈ a Kingmaker beat: a faction move, a kingdom event, a
  rival's gambit, or a fey intrusion.
