# Setting Canon — The Stolen Lands / Kingmaker   (ground truth; overrides recollection)

Consult before inventing. This is the campaign's fixed backdrop on Golarion. Spoiler-bearing
truths (the curse, the hidden hand) are marked **[HIDDEN]** — they are authored facts the **PC has
not earned**; treat them as only potential until discovered, and never steer with them.

## Premise / tone
The **Stolen Lands** are ~35,000 sq mi of unclaimed wilderness wedged between **Brevoy** (north)
and the **River Kingdoms** (south/west), long resistant to settlement. The swordlords of **Restov**
(in Rostland, southern Brevoy) issue charters to free agents to explore, pacify, and **found a new
kingdom** — a buffer that also raises Rostland's hand against the northern crown. The PCs answer
that call: from bandit-hunters to barons to monarchs of a realm. Tone: gritty frontier survival
shading into kingdom-and-realm drama, with a creeping mythic/fey threat underneath.

## Places
- **Restov** — frontier city of Rostland; the swordlords' seat; the campaign's launch point.
- **Brevoy** — fractured northern kingdom of two old realms (Issia + Rostland) yoked under
  **House Surtova**; tense, near civil war; eyes the new kingdom with calculation.
- **The River Kingdoms** — a patchwork of bandit-realms and petty states; **Pitax** (King Irovetti)
  is the relevant power, to the south-west.
- **The Stolen Lands** zones (north→south, roughly by level): **Rostland Hinterlands** (Oleg's
  Trading Post, Bokken's hut), **Greenbelt** (the starting wilderness — Thorn River, Old Sycamore,
  Sootscale Caverns, Temple of the Elk, Tuskgutter), **Tuskwater** (the Stag Lord's fort; future
  capital site), **Kamelands**, **Narlmarches** forest, **Sellen Hills** (Cradle of Lamashtu),
  **Dunsward** (Varnhold), **Nomen Heights**, **Tors of Levenies** (Vordakai's Tomb), **Hooktongue**
  (Fort Drelev, M'Botuu), **Glenebon** + **Pitax** (Irovetti's realm), **Thousand Voices** (a First
  World–touched fey wood), **Branthlend Mountains**. **Candlemere** — a haunted tower-island on the
  Tuskwater, key to the Thousandbreaths endgame.
- **The First World** — the fey demiplane that bleeds into the Greenbelt and the Thousand Voices;
  source of the campaign's uncanny.

## Factions (who wants what)
- **Aldori swordlords of Restov / Rostland** — want the Stolen Lands **claimed and loyal**
  (buffer, prestige, leverage vs House Surtova). Proud, martial, honorable; reward results.
- **House Surtova / Brevoy crown** — wants stability and no new rival; watches coldly.
- **Pitax (King Castruccio Irovetti)** — wants the Stolen Lands for himself and the PCs' kingdom
  **destroyed or annexed**; works by deniable intermediaries, propaganda, and eventually open war.
- **The Stag Lord's bandits** — want plunder, fear, and control of the Greenbelt's routes.
- **The fey / First World courts** — want their bargains honored and their woods unspoiled; act by
  alien logic. **[HIDDEN]** many serve, fear, or flee **Nyrissa**.
- **Tiger Lords** (barbarian confederation, west) and **Nomen centaurs** (Dunsward/Nomen Heights) —
  proud wilderness peoples; allies or enemies by the respect shown them.
- **Cult of the Bloom** — fey-corrupted cult tied to the Lantern King's curse; agents of ruin.
- **The PCs' kingdom & its subjects** — once founded, a faction in their own right: want safety,
  food, justice. Their discontent is **Unrest**; neglect breeds **Ruin**.

## Timeline / history
- The Stolen Lands have resisted settlement for centuries; many would-be conquerors fell here
  (the Forgotten Keep, Candlemere's order, the Nomen, the cyclopes of old Iobaria — **Vordakai**).
- Recently: rising banditry and worsening Brevic politics spur the swordlords' charters. **[HIDDEN]**
  Irovetti of Pitax secretly funds the bandit pressure to forestall a rival before it can form.
- **[HIDDEN]** Long ago the nymph queen **Nyrissa** was cursed by the demigod the **Lantern King**;
  to escape her fate she must **destroy nations**, and the nascent kingdom in the Stolen Lands sits
  atop her list. The whole campaign is, unknown to the PCs, the unfolding of her design.
